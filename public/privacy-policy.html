AgentDroid Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Applicable Version: v1.0.0
User Data Sovereignty Guarantee:

AgentDroid does NOT operate tracking servers, advertising networks, or centralized telemetry pipelines. All personal data, API keys, conversational histories, and context memories reside locally on your physical Android device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: No commercial advertising or marketing beacons.
  • Zero Location Tracking: GPS and network locations are never requested or stored.
  • Zero Contact/SMS Harvesting: Contacts and text messages are never uploaded.
  • Zero Central Telemetry: Prompts are never routed to any centralized developer server.

2. Prominent Android Accessibility Service Disclosure

AgentDroid includes an optional Android Accessibility Service (AgentDroidAccessibilityService) used by the Phone Control Agent:

  • Purpose: Allows the agent to simulate physical taps, text input, and scrolling in applications to execute user-directed goals.
  • Scope: Evaluates visible screen nodes strictly during user-initiated automation tasks.
  • Local Handling: Evaluated ephemerally in RAM; never recorded continuously, never stored on disk, and never transmitted to developer servers.
  • Revocation: Accessibility can be enabled or revoked at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility → AgentDroid.

3. Local-First Processing & Keystore Encryption

  • Hardware Keystore: Third-party API credentials are encrypted with AES-256 GCM in Android Keystore.
  • Room SQLite: Tasks, workflows, and chat histories reside in an on-device encrypted database.
  • Purge Rights: You can purge context memory tiers or wipe all app storage at any time.

4. Cloud AI Providers vs. Local Ollama

  • Cloud Providers: When using OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or Groq, prompts travel directly from your phone to that provider via HTTPS, governed by their respective privacy terms.
  • Local Private LLMs: When using Ollama or LM Studio over local Wi-Fi, zero data leaves your local network.

5. Contact Information

For questions or feedback, reach out to the open-source project maintainers at privacy@agentdroid.org or submit an issue on GitHub.
