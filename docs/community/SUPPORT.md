# Community Support & Help Channels

Need assistance configuring an AI provider, running Ollama on your local network, or developing a custom agent? Here are our official support resources.

  * [Troubleshooting Guide](file:///workspaces/agentdroid-/docs/user-guide/TROUBLESHOOTING.md)

* **Security Vulnerabilities**: Do NOT submit security issues to public trackers. Follow our [Vulnerability Reporting Protocol](file:///workspaces/agentdroid-/docs/security/VULNERABILITY_REPORTING.md).

---

## 2. Filing an Effective Bug Report

When reporting an issue, please include:
1. **Device Model & Android Version** (e.g., Pixel 7, Android 14).
2. **AgentDroid Version** (e.g., v1.0.0).
3. **AI Provider & Model** (e.g., Ollama `llama3:8b`, OpenAI `gpt-4o`).
4. **Exact Error Code** (e.g., `ERR_PROVIDER_UNREACHABLE`).
5. **Steps to Reproduce** and relevant Logcat excerpts.
