# Social Media Agent User Guide

The **Social Media Agent** in AgentDroid acts as an autonomous digital marketing assistant. It drafts platform-optimized posts, analyzes trends, crafts viral hooks, selects relevant hashtags, and structures complete multi-day campaigns.

---

## 1. Supported Platforms & Optimization Profiles

The Social Media Agent optimizes formatting, character counts, tone, and media recommendations based on the target platform:

| Platform | Format Focus | Tone Characteristics | Hashtag Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **X (Twitter)** | Short-form threads, 280-char snippets, punchy hooks | Direct, provocative, timely | 1–3 high-intent tags |
| **LinkedIn** | Long-form story posts, industry analysis, bulleted takeaways | Professional, insightful, career-oriented | 3–5 targeted industry tags |
| **Instagram** | Visual-first captions, line-spaced storytelling, calls to action | Casual, engaging, visual, emotive | 10–20 niche & community tags |
| **Reddit** | Deep dive discussions, transparent context, zero sales fluff | Authentic, candid, community-specific | Subreddit flair adherence |

---

## 2. Example Workflows & Prompts

### A. Instant Platform Adaptation
> **Prompt**: *"Take this announcement about our v1.0 release and craft tailored posts for LinkedIn and X."*
* The agent generates a thread for X featuring high-energy bullet points, and an expansive founder-perspective post for LinkedIn detailing technical breakthroughs.

### B. Hashtag & Hook Engineering
> **Prompt**: *"Generate 5 viral hooks and an optimal hashtag cluster for an Android AI development tutorial."*
* Returns structured hook variations (Curiosity Gap, Contrarian, Step-by-Step, Question, Stat-driven) alongside ranked hashtag tiers (High volume, Medium volume, Niche).

### C. Multi-Agent Social Campaign
> **Prompt**: *"Analyze our weekly product release notes, draft 3 promotional posts, and prepare a community reply template."*
* **Master Agent** delegates extraction to the Content Creation Agent, feeds text to Social Media Agent, and coordinates with Communication Agent for reply templates.

---

## 3. Human Approval & Safety

AgentDroid enforces human oversight for all outward communications:
* Posts and drafts generated by the agent remain in **Draft Review** status.
* Automated publishing or clipboard copying requires explicit confirmation if configured under Security Settings.
* The agent flags potential tone mismatches, controversial claims, or unverified facts before finalizing drafts.
