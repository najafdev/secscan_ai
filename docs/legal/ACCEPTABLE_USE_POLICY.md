# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## 1. Purpose & Scope

AgentDroid is designed as a powerful personal digital productivity assistant. This Acceptable Use Policy specifies permitted and prohibited behaviors to prevent harm, abuse, and platform compromise.

---

## 2. Prohibited Activities

You agree NOT to use AgentDroid, its agents, or its APIs to:

### A. Cybersecurity & Device Compromise
* Develop, distribute, or execute malware, spyware, ransomware, or keyloggers.
* Probe, scan, or attack undocumented local network services or unauthorized host systems.
* Attempt privilege escalation, bypass Android sandbox boundaries, or circumvent device permission dialogs.
* Intercept, extract, or brute-force cryptographic keys stored in Android Keystore.

### B. Surveillance, Harassment & Privacy Violations
* Monitor, track, or record an individual's device interactions or screen content without their explicit knowledge and consent.
* Automate targeted harassment, doxxing, stalking, or defamation campaigns.
* Scrape personally identifiable information (PII) from private databases or protected social directories.

### C. Spam & Platform Abuse
* Deploy autonomous bots to spam social media feeds, forums, or email lists.
* Automate deceptive astroturfing, fake product reviews, or artificial engagement inflation.
* Circumvent CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or platform access controls on third-party services.

### D. Fraud & Malicious Impersonation
* Impersonate any natural person, legal entity, financial institution, or government agency.
* Generate phishing lures, fraudulent invoices, or deceptive credential-harvesting communications.

---

## 3. Enforcement & Policy Violations

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy may result in:
* Immediate termination of your license under the EULA.
* Reporting of severe illegal activities (e.g. child exploitation, cyber attacks) to appropriate law enforcement authorities.
