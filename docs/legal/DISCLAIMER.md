# Operational & AI Disclaimer

**PLEASE READ THIS DISCLAIMER CAREFULLY BEFORE USING AGENTDROID.**

### 1. Artificial Intelligence Output Limitations
AgentDroid connects to generative artificial intelligence models (such as GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5, and Llama 3.2). Generative AI models are probabilistic and known to produce:
* **Factual Inaccuracies**: Models may invent citations, competitor pricing, or technical details ("hallucinations").
* **Flawed Code / Logic**: Outputted scripts, workflows, or task plans may contain bugs or misinterpret system states.
* **Unintended Tone**: Content generated by the Content Creation or Social Media agents must be independently vetted before publishing.

### 2. Native Android Automation Risks
The Phone Control Agent utilizes Android Accessibility APIs to simulate taps, text input, and gesture navigation.
* **State Misinterpretation**: If an installed app changes its UI layout, the agent may tap an unexpected control.
* **Data Loss**: While destructive actions (`DELETE_FILE`) trigger human approval gates, automated interactions in third-party apps carry inherent risks of accidental form submissions or deletions.
* **Biometric & Financial Apps**: You must **never** automate interactions inside banking, payment, or cryptocurrency wallet applications.

### 3. Third-Party Platform Compliance
Social media platforms (X, LinkedIn, Meta, Reddit) strictly enforce platform terms and rate limits prohibiting unauthorized automated spam or scraping.
* You are solely responsible for ensuring your automated campaigns comply with platform developer guidelines.
* `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` accepts zero liability for account suspensions or bans resulting from automated social media usage.

### 4. Emergency Killswitch Responsibility
AgentDroid provides an instantaneous **Emergency Killswitch** in the Android notification drawer. You are responsible for monitoring active automation and engaging the killswitch immediately if an agent exhibits unexpected behavior.
